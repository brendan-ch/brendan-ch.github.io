The CMES-Boom mobile app is a project by the non-profit organization [[Techies Without Borders]], part of the broader CMES project. The app provides a way to download and view content while offline.

I started working on the app with the team back in early 2021. The application is built with [[Angular]] on [[Capacitor by Ionic]], with some native plugins in Swift and Java.

This app is actively used in medical facilities in Uganda and Nepal, to name a few.
